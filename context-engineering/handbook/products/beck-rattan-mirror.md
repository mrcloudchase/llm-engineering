The Beck Rattan Mirror started as a sketch on our studio wall and became one of the pieces we reach for most. We chose rattan for how it feels in the hand as much as for how it looks. Customers often buy two, one for themselves and one to give away. The finish is deliberately plain, so it settles in next to what you already own. Every piece is checked by hand before it leaves the workshop, so small variations in color and texture are part of its character. If anything about it isn't right when it arrives, the help desk will sort it out. Its proportions were adjusted three times before we were happy with how it sits in a room.
