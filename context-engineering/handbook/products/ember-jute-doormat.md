The Ember Jute Doormat started as a sketch on our studio wall and became one of the pieces we reach for most. Customers often buy two, one for themselves and one to give away. The workshop pays fair wages and uses renewable power for most of its production. We chose jute for how it feels in the hand as much as for how it looks. Its proportions were adjusted three times before we were happy with how it sits in a room. Every piece is checked by hand before it leaves the workshop, so small variations in color and texture are part of its character. Its packaging is recycled cardboard and paper tape, with no plastic.
