The Wren Coir Doormat started as a sketch on our studio wall and became one of the pieces we reach for most. We chose coir for how it feels in the hand as much as for how it looks. Natural coir varies from batch to batch, so yours may differ slightly from the photographs. Every piece is checked by hand before it leaves the workshop, so small variations in color and texture are part of its character. Its packaging is recycled cardboard and paper tape, with no plastic. Its proportions were adjusted three times before we were happy with how it sits in a room. Customers often buy two, one for themselves and one to give away.
